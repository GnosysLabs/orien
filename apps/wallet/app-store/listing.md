## Description (4,000 max)

Orien Wallet is the home for ORN, the cryptocurrency built to stay safe from quantum computers.

PAYMENTS THAT ARE FINAL IN A BLINK
Send ORN to a contact, a wallet you own, or anyone on the Orien Market. Payments are final in a fraction of a second, and the fee is shown before you send.

YOUR KEYS, YOUR ORN
Orien Wallet is self-custodial. Your wallets live on your device, locked with Face ID, and only you hold the 24-word recovery phrase. View your recovery phrase any time, behind Face ID.

ALL YOUR DEVICES, NO TYPING 24 WORDS
Move a wallet to your phone or computer with a short code or QR code. Both screens show the same three emoji so you know it's really you. Contacts and wallet icons stay in step across your devices, end-to-end encrypted.

THE ORIEN MARKET
Browse what people offer for ORN: things, skills and services. Message sellers, agree the details, and pay straight from your wallet in the chat. Sellers can verify their wallet, so you know who you're paying.

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
Nine themes, from Daylight to Obsidian. Hide your balance with one tap. Lock the app with Face ID. Notifications for payments, Market messages and more, each one yours to turn on or off.

SAFE BY DESIGN
Report or block anyone from their profile or a chat. Delete your Orien Network account at any time from your profile.

Orien Wallet is made by Gnosys Labs LLC. Learn more at orien.network.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label") answers

**Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Yes. All of it is only for people who sign in to Orien Network; the wallet itself collects nothing.

| Data type | Collected | Linked to the person | Used for tracking | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contact Info: Email Address | Yes | Yes | No | App Functionality (signing in) |
| User Content: Photos or Videos | Yes | Yes | No | App Functionality (profile and listing pictures) |
| User Content: Other User Content | Yes | Yes | No | App Functionality (messages, listings, profile text) |
| Identifiers: User ID | Yes | Yes | No | App Functionality (the account) |
| Financial Info: Other Financial Info | Yes | Yes | No | App Functionality (wallet addresses a person chooses to verify on their profile) |
| Location, Health, Browsing, Search history, Contacts, Usage Data, Diagnostics, Sensitive info | No | | | |

Notes:
- Contacts and wallet icons are synced end-to-end encrypted: the server can't read them, so they don't count as collected.
- No analytics or advertising, and no tracking.
- Notification tokens are used only to deliver notifications.
