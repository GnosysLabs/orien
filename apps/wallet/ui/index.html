    Sign up or sign in

    New here? Enter your email, type the 6-digit code we send, and pick a name: that's your account. Already signed up on orien.network? Use the same email.

    Post an offer

    Offer your ORN for Bitcoin. When someone takes it, this app locks the ORN for them and runs the swap, as long as it's open.

    Smallest swap: 0.00005 BTC, more when Bitcoin is busy. Fees: the usual Orien fee to lock the ORN (0.1%, at most 1 ORN), and Bitcoin's network fee for taking the Bitcoin, out of what you receive. If a buyer never pays, your ORN come back on their own after 48 hours.

    Sell ORN

    • You need the buyer's Orien address (they find it in Orien Wallet under Swap, Buy ORN), a price you've agreed with them, and a Bitcoin address of yours to be paid at.
    • How it goes: this app locks your ORN for the buyer, you swap codes, the buyer pays the Bitcoin, and this app takes it. Usually one to two hours.
    • Fees: the usual Orien fee to lock the ORN (0.1%, at most 1 ORN), and Bitcoin's network fee for taking the Bitcoin, which comes out of what you receive. Orien Wallet itself takes nothing.
    • Smallest swap: 0.00005 BTC, more when Bitcoin is busy. If the buyer never pays, your ORN come back to you on their own after 48 hours.

    Buy ORN

    • You need Bitcoin in any Bitcoin wallet, enough for the price plus that wallet's sending fee, a Bitcoin address of yours for a refund if the swap stops, and a price you've agreed with the seller.
    • How it goes: the seller locks the ORN for you and sends an offer code, you send back a reply code and pay the Bitcoin, the seller's wallet takes it and delivers your ORN. Usually one to two hours after you pay.
    • Fees: your Bitcoin wallet's fee to pay, and the usual Orien fee (0.1%, at most 1 ORN), which comes out of the ORN you receive. Orien Wallet itself takes nothing.
    • Smallest swap: 0.00005 BTC, more when Bitcoin is busy. The wallet checks before anything is paid. If the seller never takes your Bitcoin, this app takes it back for you after about 12 hours.
