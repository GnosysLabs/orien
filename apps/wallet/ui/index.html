    Get ORN

    • Swap for it. Swap SOL or Bitcoin for ORN with other people, directly between your wallets, on the Swap screen. With SOL it takes a few minutes, and you pay from the SOL wallet you already have, such as Phantom.
    • Mine it on a computer. Install Orien Wallet on your Mac, Windows or Linux computer, open this same wallet there with its recovery phrase, and press Mine. Your computer earns a share of each hour's new ORN.
    • Be paid in ORN. Give someone your address and they can send you ORN from their wallet. Payments arrive in seconds.

    Orien Wallet for computers is at orien.network.
